Tunables for fit_ccf_rv_bisector() (traditional CCF bisector RV).

Follows the NEID-DRP bisector construction (percentile cuts through the CCF in velocity space; see NEID algorithms § CCF Bisector Calculation). Radial velocity and its uncertainty are a weighted mean of bisector midpoints \((v_\mathrm{left}+v_\mathrm{right})/2\) at multiple depth percentiles: with per-cut midpoint uncertainties \(e_i\) and weights \(w_i\),

\[\hat{v} = \frac{\sum_i w_i v_i}{\sum_i w_i},\qquad \sigma_{\hat{v}} = \frac{\sum_i w_i e_i}{\sum_i w_i}.\]

(The uncertainty is the weighted average of the cut uncertainties, not an independent-measurement reduction of those errors.) Cut weights are the core performance weight only; per-cut \(e_i\) comes from CCF-error propagation when informative, otherwise from the smooth-profile discretization estimate.

calc_bisector_inverse_slope(vel_grid, ccf, ccf_err=None, n_levels=100, quant_shallow=(0.1, 0.4), quant_core=(0.6, 0.9))[source]

Bisector Inverse Slope (BIS) of a CCF, after Queloz et al. (2001).

Thin wrapper over the njit core (_calc_bis). ccf_err is optional: it is used only to propagate the velocity uncertainties, never the velocity values themselves. When ccf_err is None the bisector velocities are computed normally and all three error outputs (bis_err, shallow_err, core_err) are returned as NaN, since there is no uncertainty information to propagate.

See _calc_bis for the full parameter and algorithm description. In brief, the bisector midpoints are averaged over two depth-fraction windows measured from the continuum toward the extremum (orientation-invariant for both absorption dips and emission peaks):

BIS = v_shallow - v_core

with shallow near the continuum and core near the core.

Parameters:

  • vel_grid (ndarray) – Velocity grid (ascending) and CCF values.

  • ccf (ndarray) – Velocity grid (ascending) and CCF values.

  • ccf_err (ndarray | None) – 1-sigma uncertainties on ccf. Optional; if None, the error outputs are NaN.

  • n_levels (int) – Number of evenly spaced depth levels sampled across the 0-1 range.

  • quant_shallow (tuple[float, float] | None) – Lower/upper depth fractions (0-1, measured from the continuum) bounding the shallow (near-continuum) region used for v_shallow. Defaults to (0.10, 0.40).

  • quant_core (tuple[float, float]) – Lower/upper depth fractions (0-1, measured from the continuum) bounding the core (near-core) region used for v_core. Defaults to (0.6, 0.90).

Returns:

  • bis (float) – The bisector inverse slope (v_shallow - v_core). NaN if not measurable.

  • bis_err (float) – Propagated 1-sigma uncertainty on bis (quadrature sum of shallow_err and core_err). NaN if not measurable or if ccf_err was None.

  • v_shallow (float) – Mean bisector velocity over the shallow (near-continuum) region.

  • shallow_err (float) – 1-sigma uncertainty on v_shallow. NaN if not measurable or if ccf_err was None.

  • v_core (float) – Mean bisector velocity over the core (near-core) region.

  • core_err (float) – 1-sigma uncertainty on v_core. NaN if not measurable or if ccf_err was None.

  • levels, bis_curve (ndarray) – Depth fractions and bisector velocities at each successfully bracketed level, sampled across the full 0-1 depth range. Paired element-for- element.

fit_ccf_rv_bisector(v_grid_m_s, ccf, v_step_m_s, *, ccf_err=None, config=None, return_diagnostics=False, gaussian_seed_mean_m_s=None)[source]

Radial velocity (m/s) from a traditional CCF bisector analysis.

At each depth percentile between baseline and peak, finds the left and right wing velocities where the CCF crosses that flux level and takes their midpoint. The RV estimate and its uncertainty are a performance-weighted mean of midpoints over config.rv_percentile_lo … config.rv_percentile_hi, with Gaussian weight emphasizing the CCF core (50% depth). The reported σ is the weighted average of per-cut midpoint uncertainties (\(\sum_i w_i e_i\,/\,\sum_i w_i\)), not an independent-cut \(1/\sqrt{N}\) reduction.

Also computes bisector inverse slope (BIS) following NEID-DRP: average midpoints in the top (60–90%) minus bottom (10–40%) sections, with the same weighted-mean uncertainty on each section.

Parameters:

  • v_grid_m_s (np.ndarray) – Velocity samples (m/s), need not be uniform.

  • ccf (np.ndarray) – CCF values on the same grid.

  • v_step_m_s (float) – Effective finest velocity spacing (m/s) for uncertainty floors; use effective_ccf_v_step_for_fit() on v_grid_m_s.

  • ccf_err (np.ndarray or None, optional) – Per-sample CCF uncertainty for wing-crossing error propagation.

  • config (CCFBisectorConfig or None, optional) – Bisector percentile ranges and weighting.

  • return_diagnostics (bool, optional) – If True, return (rv, rv_err, diag) with per-cut midpoints, BIS, etc.

  • gaussian_seed_mean_m_s (float or None, optional) – When set (typically the Gaussian CCF fit center), the bisector peak index is the sample nearest this velocity (with a small local refine). Do not re-search argmax(|CCF − baseline|) over a multi-km/s window: a global contrast search can lock onto a window edge when the core has near-zero contrast relative to an unweighted continuum.

Returns:

  • rv_m_s, rv_err_m_s (float) – Weighted bisector RV and combined uncertainty (m/s).

  • diag (dict, optional) – Present when return_diagnostics=True.
